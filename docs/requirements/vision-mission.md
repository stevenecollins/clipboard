# Vision & Mission

## Vision Statement

To create the universal bookmarking solution that empowers users to capture, organize, and revisit their digital discoveries across all platforms and devices, without losing context or source attribution.

## Mission Statement

We're building Clipboards to solve the fundamental problem of digital content preservation in an ephemeral internet age. Our mission is to:

- **Preserve Context**: Always maintain the source link alongside visual content
- **Enable Universal Access**: Work seamlessly across mobile, desktop, and web platforms
- **Protect Privacy**: Keep all user content private by default
- **Simplify Organization**: Provide intuitive board-based organization without complexity
- **Ensure Permanence**: Create a reliable vault for important digital discoveries

## Core Problem We're Solving

### Current Pain Points
- Pinterest locks saves to their ecosystem and removes source links
- Screenshots lose context and source attribution  
- Bookmarks are scattered across different browsers and devices
- Social media and marketplace content disappears or becomes inaccessible
- No unified solution exists for cross-platform content saving

### Our Solution
A cross-platform application that captures both images and source URLs, organizes them in private boards, and syncs across all user devices while maintaining complete privacy and ownership.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users
- **Digital Researchers**: People who save articles, images, and resources for projects
- **Shoppers**: Users who want to save products from multiple platforms with links intact  
- **Content Curators**: Individuals building collections of inspiration or references
- **Students & Professionals**: Those who need to organize and reference digital materials

### Use Cases
- Saving Pinterest-style inspiration boards with working source links
- Collecting products from multiple e-commerce sites for comparison
- Building research databases with proper source attribution
- Creating mood boards that maintain clickable references
- Archiving social media content before it disappears

### Short-term (6 months)
- 1,000+ active users

- Sub-2-second capture time

### Long-term (12-18 months)
- 10,000+ active users
- 100,000+ items saved monthly
- 90%+ user retention after first week

## Competitive Advantage

1. **Dual Capture**: Saves both image and source link simultaneously
2. **Privacy First**: All content remains private to the user
3. **Universal Platform Support**: Works on mobile, desktop, and web
4. **Instant Sync**: Real-time synchronization across all devices
5. **Source Preservation**: Never lose the original link to saved content
